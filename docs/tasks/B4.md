## Required behavior
The owner supplies the first list. `follow_wallets` is loaded with `ctl lists load follow_wallets file.txt --token T` (one address per line, optional `# label` after it), and again whenever the owner updates it: each load is a new version, applied from now on. No scraping and no new dependency; nothing is fetched from third-party sites.
- The Scout gives listed wallets the role `follow` at once (the hot set reloads on `lists.loaded`, not on the next timer); their buy writes `coin.watched` with reason `follow` and the wallet.
- Analysis is the Index's own: a listed wallet's rollups and copy scores build from the trades we see (`copy_net`, `copy_win_lcb`, `n_coins`; `docs/PIPELINE.md` section 3). Until it has enough coins the score is shown as unproven, not high or low. History from before the wallet was listed is not fetched here.
- A listed wallet that the Index later marks `sniper`, `bundler` or `dev` keeps its role but its buys write `coin.watched` with a note, so the owner can prune the list. Pruning is the owner's act.
- The `follow` feed source (B2) reads callouts by those wallets.
