## Current behavior
Observed: the Feed Reader reads one source, `/home-feed/new` (`services/core/src/senses/feed-reader.ts:160`), and writes every callout it sees (`feed-reader.ts:194`, `:199`). Nothing is filtered, counted as rejected or sampled.

## Required behavior
Sources come from `config/feeds.json`, each with its own cursor in `tape.feed_cursors`, and only those our own research documents (`docs/research/pumpfun-endpoints.md`): `recent` (`/home-feed/new`, as now), `hot` (`/home-feed`, ranked) and `follow` (the NATS `followingFeed` subject, plus B4's wallets). `trending` and `top_traders` are left out until we have recorded their endpoint and shape ourselves; nothing is taken from the upstream repos for sources. A pure `passes(callout, filters)` in `services/core/src/senses/filters.ts` checks chain, minimum position in USD and market cap at call before the callout is written. Rejected callouts are counted per reason in stats, and `FILTER_SAMPLE_PCT` of them are written as `filter.sampled` (reason, source, callout). Sampling is by a hash of the callout id, so a replay samples the same ones.

## Invariants
Filters drop only at the door; nothing already on the Tape is removed. `via: history` callouts pass the same filters. A source is added only after we fetched it ourselves and recorded the shape in `docs/research/`; never guessed, never copied from another repo. Every source keeps the Feed Reader's backoff and `Retry-After` handling, and a source that is rate limited is skipped for that poll and counted, not retried in a loop.
