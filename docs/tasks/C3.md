# C3: Sizing reads each family's calibration map, never the raw score

Mode: C (contracts, schema) · Writer: top agent · Depends on: C2, C4, C5 · PLAN row: C3

## Current behavior
Observed: `mind/dsl.ts:160-162` sized by Kelly on the raw score as if it were a win chance, and `mind/desk.ts` used that Kelly whenever a strategy was `full`. No calibration map existed; positions did not keep their entry score; signal confidence never reached the score.

## Required behavior
- `mind/calibrate.ts`: per family, closed decisions binned by entry score in equal-count bins of `CALIB_MIN_PER_BIN` (20); win share and mean net made non-decreasing (pool adjacent violators).
- Each allocation round rebuilds the maps; a changed map gets a version in `mind.calibration` and a `calibration.map` event with its counts.
- Until a family's map has 3 bins every entry uses `probe_order_sol`, whatever its stage. After: the bin's mean net must beat the costs; a `full` strategy sizes by fractional Kelly on the bin's win share, capped at `KELLY_CAP` (0.25), then the kernel share, then the owner's limits.
- A score term whose signal confidence is under `TERM_MIN_CONF` (0.3, my pick) counts as missing.
- `mind.positions.score` and `mind.intents.score` keep the entry score.
