## Required behavior
A core consumer, the **escalation gate** (`services/core/src/mind/escalate.ts`), decides stage by stage, from numbers computed in code (`docs/SLOW-MIND.md` section 3):
1. Eligible: in play by `precall`, `smart_buy`, or a callout in the last minute (the in-play reason C2 records).
2. Unsure: the calibrated win chance from the family's map (C3) is between `ESC_P_LOW` (0.35) and `ESC_P_HIGH` (0.65), or two fast signals disagree by more than `ESC_DISAGREE`.
3. Value of information: P(decision changes) × expected net change > the stage's cost in SOL (`SLOW_STAGE<n>_USD_PER_RUN` ÷ `SOL_USD`). Every number is computed in code; write the formula in the doc.
4. Room: quota and caps (D2 adds the USD caps; until then, the per-hour quotas). A denied escalation is written as deferred (D5 picks it up).
5. **Control split:** a stable hash of the mint (`sha256(mint) mod 2`) sends half the eligible coins to `control`. Control coins get a `jev.escalated` with `gate_reasons` including `control` and **no job**, so they can be compared later. Record the arm on every event.
6. **Hot set:** every `HOT_SET_EVERY_S` (90) of Tape time, the top `HOT_SET_SIZE` (5) coins by pre-call chance × caller quality get a stage 1 grant, even without a new trigger.
7. Stage 1 = BettaFish nano coin run (`job: synthesis_coin` with preset `nano`); stage 2 = lite plus a small swarm; stage 3 = owner preset only (never automatic).

Add `synthesis.coin`, `swarm.wave_p` and `mood.feed` to the signal catalog as System Two kinds. The validator refuses them in `fast` strategies and requires `missing: skip` (`docs/STRATEGY.md` section 2).

Contract changes (top agent, frozen before writing): add `stage: 0|1|2|3`, `arm: "run"|"control"` and `deferred: boolean` to `JevEscalated`; add the `ESC_*`, `HOT_SET_*` and `SLOW_STAGE*_USD_PER_RUN` keys to `.env.example`.
