# C10: Caller records carry the fields the pre-call score needs

Mode: B · Writer: top agent · Depends on: C9 · PLAN row: C10

## Current behavior
Observed: `analyzers/precall.ts` `record()` (was :37-56) returned buys, called and lags only; each buyer's score was its shrunk call rate, and the top buyer was the one most likely to call, whatever the copy would earn. No `post_call_net`, `dump_rate`, `creator_share` or `n_calls`.

## Required behavior
- `callerRecord`: per closed qualifying buy, whether the same wallet called within `PRECALL_HORIZON_MIN`, the lag, our net (entry at the first trade after their buy; exit into their callout, or at the last trade of the horizon with no callout; `INDEX_ROUND_TRIP_COST` charged), and whether they sold within the horizon after calling. Plus `n_calls` and the calls on coins whose `coin.watched` names them as creator.
- `callerFields`: `buy_to_call_rate`, `post_call_net`, `miss_net`, each shrunk to the population by `PRECALL_PRIOR_N`; `edge` = rate x (post - 1) - (1 - rate) x (1 - miss); `dump_rate`, `creator_share`, `n_calls`, `lag_med_s`.
- `precall.callout_p` names the buyer with the highest `edge` and carries every field, plus `edge_norm` (0.5 + 2.5 x edge, clamped) as a score input. The `pre_call` seeds score mostly on `edge_norm` and skip a caller whose `creator_share` is 0.5 or more.
