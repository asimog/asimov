# Later

Scope that is planned but far off. Active docs describe only the firm being built now. Items move from here into `PLAN.md` when the owner says so or a milestone unlocks them.

## Milestones

Growth unlocks by firm NAV, with one health condition: the books reconcile and no Signer incident is open. A broken reconciliation or a Signer incident drops the firm back one milestone. Values are defaults the owner may change.

| Milestone | NAV | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Start | 0.2 SOL | Paper strategies, tiny live callout trades, callouts and updates |
| M1 | 30 days of its own runway | Thinking agents on their own budget; Builder on this repo |
| M2 | 2 SOL | Larger sizes within limits; slow presets up to `lite`; Builder rebuilds cancerhawk.org |
| M3 | 10 SOL | Live forecasting on prediction markets; polymyths.com |
| M4 | 100 SOL | BTC and ETH desk; hyperkaon.com; buys its own compute |
| M5 | 1,000 SOL | Holder payouts and seats; larger simulations; new venues |
