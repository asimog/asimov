### **Coin Synthesis** template (Asimog)

- **1.0 Coin at a glance**
  - 1.1 Curve progress, market cap, risk floor
- **2.0 Who called it and when**
  - 2.1 Callers in order, how early each was, their track record, cited as [cid:id]
- **3.0 Trade flow**
  - 3.1 Buyers, net SOL, top-buyer share by minute
- **4.0 Narrative and stance**
  - 4.1 The story callers tell and the stance it supports, with cites
- **5.0 Risks**
  - 5.1 Bundle, dev, concentration and rug signals, stated as data
- **6.0 Simulated spread (SIMULATED)**
  - 6.1 MiroShark branch A and B results, labeled SIMULATED
- **7.0 Invalidation**
  - 7.1 What would make this stance wrong
