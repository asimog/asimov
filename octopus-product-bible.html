It reads every callout. It simulates every caller.
It buys first, calls second, and proves it.

OCTOPUS is an autonomous trading creature living inside Pump.fun callouts. BettaFish tells it what the crowd feels right now. MiroShark simulates what the callers will do next. When the simulation says a wave of callouts is coming and the token is still early, it buys, posts its own callout with the position disclosed, and calls its exits live.

    NERVOUS SYSTEM · EVENT TAPE
      01 · The thesis−10m

      Pump.fun callouts are Keynes's beauty contest, played live

      Keynes said winning a beauty contest means picking the face other judges will pick. Memecoins work the same way. Price follows attention, and attention follows callers. So the edge is to predict the callers, not the price, get positioned before the wave, and let the wave do the work.

      Motive

      Profitable trades and callouts

      The octopus exists to make money and to make calls that make its followers money. Everything else, including the lore, the 3D tank and the hunts, serves that.

      Edge

      It knows who calls next

      Every tracked caller has a Shadow: a simulated twin fit on their real history. The swarm of Shadows forecasts the callout wave before it arrives.

      Proof

      Every call is a public record

      Entry, size, exits and outcome are logged on-chain and on the ledger. Three to four weeks of record before the coin exists.

      • How callouts workA callout names a token and notifies the caller's followers. Callouts show up in feeds and rankings. The callout terms set no limit on how often you can call. The six-hour figure in early press coverage is not in the terms, and I was wrong to build on it.
      • Position disclosureSection 5.2(c) asks callers to disclose material positions. OCTOPUS puts its position on every card, which also happens to be its best trust signal.
      • Account modelOne account, clearly an AI. No reward chasing, no extra accounts, no bought engagement. You have confirmed the setup with Pump.fun.
      02 · The core loop−80m

      The Tide: eight beats, running forever

      SENSE

      Crawl the callout feed, profiles and comments. Resolve the mint. Snapshot curve, holders, flows.

      REMEMBER

      Bitemporal memory: when it happened, when we saw it. Caller graph in Neo4j.

      FEEL

      BettaFish reads X, Telegram, comments and meme images. Outputs crowd mood and narrative heat.

      SIMULATE

      MiroShark runs the Shadows forward in two branches: with and without the octopus calling.

      PRICE

      Turn the predicted wave into SOL inflow, then into price on the bonding curve. Size with fractional Kelly.

      BUY → CALL

      Buy first. Post the callout seconds later, with the position and exit ladder on it.

      RIDE

      Every exit is posted live as a follow-up, so followers ride the same tentacle.

      MOLT

      Score both PnLs. Retrain Shadows. Promote a new brain only if it wins on unseen days.

      Two speeds. Reflex (under 2 s): SENSE → PRICE on every event, all deterministic code. Deep (1–5 min): FEEL and SIMULATE run on tokens that just entered Stirring or Ignition. Fast strategies such as the bellwether follow skip Deep entirely.

      03 · Two fish in the brain−200m

      BettaFish feels the present. MiroShark simulates the future.

      BettaFish · "What do people think right now?"

      Each of its engines gets a callout-native job. Its ForumEngine becomes the room where the tentacles argue before a trade.

      QueryEngineSearches X, Telegram, and the web for the token's ticker, contract address and narrative.
      MediaEngineReads the token image, meme variants, and livestream thumbnails. Memecoins sell on pictures, so a multimodal read matters.
      InsightEngineMines OCTOPUS's own database: callout text, callout comments, caller histories.
      ForumEngineModerator plus tentacle agents (Hunter, Skeptic, Forensic, Quant) debating the trade in rounds.
      ReportEngineWrites dossiers, the daily Tide Report, and Hunt Board exposés.
      Sentiment modelsIts fine-tuned classifiers give the Skin score: fear, greed, FOMO, disgust, per token and for the whole feed.
      MiroShark (MiroFish lineage) · "What will people do next?"

      MiroShark already has the parts a callout simulator needs: agents that post and trade, a market, live news injection, and forked branches. Use MiroFish upstream (OASIS core) when you need thousand-agent swarms.

      Graph buildOntology from the token, its callers and their follow graph, stored in Neo4j (which MiroShark already uses).
      AgentsShadows of real callers, plus follower archetypes: sniper, bundler, degen follower, diamond hand, paper hand, exit farmer.
      EnvironmentA new platform module that mimics the Pump.fun callout feed and bonding curve, next to its X and Reddit modules.
      MarketIts prediction market prices wave contracts such as "≥5 tracked callers within 2h". The market price is the forecast.
      Director ModeInjects "OCTOPUS calls now" into a fork. Branch B minus Branch A gives the octopus's own impact.
      ChatVisitors can talk to any Shadow on the website: "Shadow of @caller, would you call $SQID?"
      Tide Futures · MiroShark branches
      Simulated sample. 60 runs per branch.
      Keeping the swarm honest
      • Numbers come from code. A Shadow's call probability comes from its fitted model. Its LLM voice explains the number and can nudge it a little, never replace it.
      • Every simulation is a forecast. Each run logs its prediction, and real callers score it two hours later.
      • Shadow fidelity is public. How often each Shadow predicted its caller correctly. Bad Shadows get retrained or retired.
      • Callout text is untrusted. Shadows read structured fields, so a callout cannot prompt-inject the octopus.

      Distribution

      HUNT
      Hawkes cascade · callouts excite callouts
      Simulated sample.
      What each organ does
      • Gaussian HMM, later a semi-Markov HSMM on 1-minute bars: callout count, distinct callers, caller-quality-weighted calls, echo ratio, net SOL flow, unique buyers, top-10 share, curve progress. The live posterior colors every token.
      • Hawkes process. Each callout raises the odds of the next one, then decays. The branching ratio n says whether a cascade dies out (n < 1) or explodes (n ≈ 1+). This is the fastest wave signal, and MiroShark refines it.
      • Change-point detection (BOCPD) spots regime breaks within a few bars. That is the "tremor" that triggers an emergency exit.
      • Bonding-curve math. The curve is constant-product with virtual reserves, so predicted SOL inflow maps to a price. Expected value of a trade becomes arithmetic, not vibes.
      05 · The profit engine−700m

      Five strategy tentacles, one treasury

      Each strategy is its own tentacle with its own capital. A Thompson-sampling bandit moves capital toward the tentacles that are earning and away from the ones that are not. Followers get the same exits the octopus takes, so octopus PnL and follower PnL rise together. That is what keeps people following.

      Sizing

      Fractional Kelly from the simulated win probability and payoff, capped by curve depth so the octopus's own buy never moves price more than a set slippage.

      Exit callouts

      Every sell posts a follow-up within seconds: "Took 25% at 2.1x. Holding 50%." Copy-traders ride the exits too. This is the most addictive feature for followers.

      Feeding (after the coin)

      Every minute creator fees are swept to the treasury and split between the trading war chest and buybacks. On screen, the octopus eats a glowing plankton burst, linked to the transaction.

      Ledger metric

      Octopus PnL

      Net of fees and slippage, per tentacle.

      Ledger metric

      Follower PnL

      A follower who buys 30 s after the call and copies the exits.

      Ledger metric

      Wave hit rate

      Did the predicted callers actually show up?

      Ledger metric

      Calibration

      Brier score of p(wave) against a naive baseline.

      07 · The hunt−1,500m

      Feared by bad callers, followed by everyone else

      Hunting lives on the website's Hunt Board and in callout comments. OCTOPUS never makes a callout post that calls out a person.

      Echo rings

      Clusters of callers who always call within minutes of one origin account. Six calls count as one signal. Drawn as an ink cloud in the Reef.

      Late callers

      Callers whose median opportunity age is high: they call after the move. Their Shadow card shows "calls at +140% on average".

      Dump patterns

      Wallets linked to a caller that sell into that caller's own follower wave. Transaction links and a confidence score.

      Ink notes in comments

      Under a callout, the octopus may leave one short data note: "Opportunity age 42 min, +180% before this call. 4 of 6 earlier callers trace to one origin." Data only, one note per callout, and only above an evidence threshold, so the notes stay rare and worth reading.

      Models

      A fast, cheap Claude model for Shadow voices and forum turns. The strongest Claude model for the moderator, the Skeptic and dossiers. Both repos accept any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, so route them through one gateway.

      Hosting

      Railway for the always-on Watcher, workers, Postgres, Neo4j and Redis. MiroShark already ships Railway configs. The web app can go on Vercel or Railway.

      Keys

      The signer runs as its own service with hard size and loss caps. Agents send trade intents to it and never hold keys. That way a bad prompt can cost a forecast, never the treasury.
